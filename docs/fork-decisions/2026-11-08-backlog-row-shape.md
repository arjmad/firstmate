2026-11-08: Arjun adopted the shared-todo-structure-1108 recommendation in the helm ("adopt"), relayed by the main firstmate, for the shape of a backlog row shared between him and the fleet.
Decision: a hold uses one of four kinds strictly (captain, external, future, parked) and always carries a date, a real deadline is a `Due:` body line kept apart from the back-on date, titles carry no date, a captain deferral replaces the row's deferral record instead of adding to it, and rows he moves together share a `Batch:` tag that one command defers at once.
Captain exception: a new captain call filed without a back-on date stays live and gets a `Due:` line of the next day (or the date he names), because tasks-axi lifts a hold on its back-on date, so a new call dated today would read as ready work and draw an overdue wake; his later deferral gives it a back-on date as before.
Why: 41 appended deferral blocks were 17% of the open backlog, 15 holds had no date against his 2026-11-04 rule that every call gets one, real deadlines lived only in prose, and 27 of the 41 deferrals moved the same 9 office rows one at a time.
The batch tag is a body line because tasks-axi 0.2.6 has no labels; adopting upstream labels or proposing a due field to tasks-axi waits for his word.
