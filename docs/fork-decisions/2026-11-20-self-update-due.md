2026-11-20: Arjun approved audit item a1-01 at the helm, which carries the [2026-09-29 self-update ruling](2026-09-29-firstmate-self-update.md) into machinery.
Decision: the watcher of a main home raises a `check: firstmate self-update due:` wake once per landed firstmate head that its code root lacks, after no task of that home works on firstmate, and again daily while it stays due, and teardown names such a head where landing ends; `/updatefirstmate` stays the step firstmate runs, and nothing runs it automatically.
Why: the helm and the Fafnir secondmate ran 38 to 41 commits behind main for two days because no skill or check named the step, and the fleet sync skips the firstmate home.
