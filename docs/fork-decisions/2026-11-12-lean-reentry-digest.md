Decision: the session-start backlog section prints in full every in-flight and blocked row, and a held row only when it is a live captain call or is due or back within 2 days.
A live captain call is a captain hold whose `bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh` hold_bucket is `live`, read from the same structured fields: not blocked, not dated after today, and not an undated hold 14 or more days old.
Every other held row is counted in one line per group, naming `bin/fm-tasks-axi.sh list --state held` with the hold fields, which lists them all.
Captain calls group as the [2026-11-11 calls grouping](2026-11-11-calls-grouped-by-what-it-takes.md) groups them (board, place, quick, hands), other holds by hold kind, each split by `Batch:` tag or repo, with the group's next back-on date.
A held row that the in-flight or blocked group already lists is not printed again, and every group asks for `hold_until`, so that row still shows its back-on date there.
Ready queued rows keep id, kind, repo and due date, bounded as before, and a ready row due within 2 days is always shown.
A title tasks-axi cut at 80 characters ends in `...` instead of its long per-row pointer, which the section prints once.
Status tails print 3 lines per task instead of 5, an orphan log prints its newest line, and the full-log path is named once per section.
The manual and fallback title-line listing and the spill read-check path are unchanged; the read-once contract keeps its shape and now names the counted held rows and the status-log legend.
Why: on Heimdall's main home the held group was 32 KB of a 104 KB re-entry digest, most rows could not act within days, and the helm re-read every row after each compaction.
If this goes, the startup listing is no longer a full recovery list of held rows; the counted rows stay one printed command away.
